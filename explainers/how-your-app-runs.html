How your app runs

What you are looking at. The Day Planner is six files, and here they are built as one bench machine. index.html is the frame: the bed of beams, the column and the console that hold every part in its place, including the knobs you turn. app.css is the paint and panel layout: the painted plate on the console that decides where the window and knobs sit and how they look. app.js is the motor: it reads the knobs, takes a card from the hopper, turns the gearbox and writes the answer in the window. logic.js is a sealed gearbox of pure functions (a pure function is a rule that gives the same answer every time it gets the same input, and touches nothing else). sample-data.js is the fuel hopper: a magazine of task cards, one card per task. tests/ is the test rig bolted in front of the gearbox: it pushes in a card whose answer is already known and checks what comes out.

Try it. Pull the lever (tap it, press Space, or use the Pull lever button under the figure) and watch one card go through: each part lights up in turn and the trace under the figure shows the value at that stage. Tap any part of the machine to light it and see which file it is. Step through the stages at your own pace with ← → or the Back and Next buttons, change the hours you have today with ↑ ↓, the right-hand knob or the Hours buttons, run only the test rig with T or the small button on the rig, and go back to the start with Home or Reset.
